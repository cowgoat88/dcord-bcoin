Tools

Tap/drag an empty tile to start construction — bulldoze first to replace something. New builds sit as a site consuming Lumber/Concrete from the stockpile over several days; short on materials and it stalls (red outline) until a Lumber Yard/Quarry restocks it. Lumber and Concrete are capped — build a Warehouse to raise the cap. Industrial zones convert raw materials into Goods (also capped), which auto-export for cash — Warehouses also raise export capacity, so they're the real income lever, not taxes. Lumber Yards/Quarries built next to each other each produce more (clustering). Zoned tiles grow once built and powered, as long as they're within a road's service radius (not necessarily touching it — bigger with road upgrades). Houses also need a nearby Farm: food fades with distance from the farm (orange outline = house isn't getting enough), so population growth is capped by farm coverage, not just power/road/demand. Upgrade tool: tap a built Power Plant/Lumber Yard/Quarry/Road/Farm to spend money+materials on higher output/radius and lower upkeep (2 tiers). Use Pan + Zoom to work at a comfortable tile size on a small screen.
