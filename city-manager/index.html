Tools

Tap/drag an empty tile to start construction — bulldoze first to replace something. New builds sit as a site consuming Lumber/Concrete from the stockpile over several days; short on materials and it stalls (red outline) until a Lumber Yard/Quarry restocks it. Zoned tiles only grow once built, adjacent to a road, and powered. Use Pan + Zoom to work at a comfortable tile size on a small screen.
