HOW TO PLAY

The round

Draft a role (fewest points picks first) → plot orders against a frozen galaxy → lock → thirty seconds of war play out with nobody touching anything → score. First to 20 points, or the most after 18 rounds.

Orders

Each costs one order point (3 a round, +1 per 5 positions). Tap your position, then a target, to send 25–100% of its ships. Support lends half a position's strength to a neighbour's fight without moving (cut if it is attacked). Hold digs in: 25% harder to take, but it sends nothing. Upgrade builds faster. Fleets only travel through your own ground and fight any rival fleet they meet in a lane.

Forecast and replay

Drag the slider to watch the round as it would go if no rival did anything. Replay shows the last round again.

Points

Holding the Throne at the centre scores every round: 1 early, 2 mid-skirmish, 3 late. Each round you also score your best public objective once and your secret once (◎ Objectives). The Throne's Doomstar fires at a rival once its Relays have charged it.

Fog of war

You see your positions and one lane out, two from Relays. Dimmed positions with a ~ show what you saw last.

The council

One law a round (⚖ Council). Vote, and add influence (1 a round, +1 per Relay) to swing it; it acts as the round resolves. Pacts: two rounds of peace and shared sight. Breaking one makes you an Oathbreaker.

Technology

⚙ Tech: twelve technologies in four branches, one a round, each needing the one above it. Every faction also bends one rule; see the start screen.

Campaign

Four skirmishes against the same rivals. Places earn 5/3/2/1; between skirmishes everyone drafts a lasting legacy, the trailing seat first. Progress is saved in this browser.
